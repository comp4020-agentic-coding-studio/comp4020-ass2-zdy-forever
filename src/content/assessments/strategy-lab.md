## The brief

> Build and defend two trade plans — one swing, one day-trading — that
> survive someone else poking holes in them.

Strategy Lab is where chart reading (Week 4) and risk (Week 5) turn into
actual plans, tested against both the swing (Week 6) and day-trading (Week 7)
styles rather than just one. The mark is not for picking setups that
"worked" — all data here is simulated, and the task does not ask you to
prove either plan would have made money — it is for showing the reasoning
and the risk control around each one.

## What you submit

One document containing two trade plans, each on its own simulated setup:

- **A swing trade plan** (roughly 400–600 words) and
- **A day trade plan** (roughly 400–600 words),

each separating observation, assumption, entry, invalidation, position size
(with the calculation shown, not just a stop-loss price), and exit. Where a
plan uses a moving average or VWAP, treat it as one piece of supporting
evidence alongside the observation — not a standalone buy or sell signal.
Each plan must state at least one condition under which it should not be
traded at all. Simulated trades and data must be labelled as simulated
throughout.

## Before you submit

Re-read each plan as if you were the person poking holes in it: does the
risk section stand on its own, or does it quietly depend on the trade going
the way you hoped?
