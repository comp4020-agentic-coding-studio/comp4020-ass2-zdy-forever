Idris runs the sessions where a concept from lecture gets put through its
paces on a simulated order, chart or contract until a student can trace it
themselves. He teaches the even-numbered weeks — order mechanics, chart
reading, swing trading, margin and leverage, options — and marks most of the
in-session work that leads into them.

Reachable during any session, or by email for anything that can't wait until
the next one; expect a reply within two working days. For questions about a
grade already returned, email first — Marisol is the point of escalation if
that doesn't resolve it.
