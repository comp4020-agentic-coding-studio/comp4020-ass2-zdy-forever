A synthesis session: drafting time on the Personal Market Rulebook, working
back through every instrument the semester covered rather than learning
anything new.

## What you're given

Every trade plan and calculation you've produced since Week 5: position
sizing, margin and margin calls, futures margin, option payoffs, and the
crypto comparison — plus the rulebook's required elements from the
assessment brief.

## What you do

Work instrument by instrument in reverse, from crypto back to the first
stock order in Week 2, drafting a rule for each: whether and how you'd use
it, and at what size. Check the rules against each other, not just each
instrument on its own — an account-level risk cap has to hold even when more
than one position is open at once. Draft at least one condition under which
you'd stop trading altogether.

## What you produce

A working draft of the Personal Market Rulebook, with a rule for every
instrument the course covered and at least one stop-trading condition
written down.

## Why it matters

What leaves this session is a draft, not the final submission — the
Rulebook itself is due at the end of the teaching period, and this is the
only session built to give you time on it.
