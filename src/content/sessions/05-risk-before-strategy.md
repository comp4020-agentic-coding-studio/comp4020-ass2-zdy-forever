A worked-calculation session: the position-sizing and expected-value
arithmetic from this week's lecture, done by hand before the simulated
platform does it for you.

## What you're given

A handful of simulated scenarios, each with an account balance, a fixed
risk-per-trade percentage, an entry price and a stop price — plus, for the
expected-value half, a win rate and an average win/loss size.

## What you do

For each scenario, calculate the stop distance, the dollar risk budget, and
the resulting position size by hand first, then check it against the
simulated platform's own calculator. Separately, calculate expected value for
a setup and state whether it's positive even though it might lose more often
than it wins.

## What you produce

Worked position-sizing calculations for every scenario, plus one
expected-value calculation with a written line on what a positive EV does
and doesn't guarantee about any single trade.

## Why it matters

Every trade plan from Week 6 onward is expected to show this calculation,
not just a stop-loss price — this is the toolkit, not a one-off exercise.
