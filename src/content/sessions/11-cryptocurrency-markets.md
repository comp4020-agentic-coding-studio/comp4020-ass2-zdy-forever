A comparison-and-risk-analysis session: checking how much of what you
already know about stock exchanges and leverage still holds once the
instrument is crypto.

## What you're given

A description of one simulated crypto exchange's structure (custody model,
trading hours, standardisation) and a simulated leveraged long crypto
position with its own-money amount and leverage ratio.

## What you do

Compare the crypto exchange's structure point-by-point against Week 2's
stock-exchange mechanics, naming what's the same and what genuinely isn't
(custody, in particular, has no stock-market equivalent). Then apply Week
5's position-sizing method to the simulated crypto position and calculate
the approximate liquidation price.

## What you produce

A short structural comparison table, plus the position-sizing and
liquidation-price calculation for the simulated position.

## Why it matters

This is the last hands-on session before the semester turns to synthesis in
Week 12 — the risk method doesn't change just because the instrument is new.
