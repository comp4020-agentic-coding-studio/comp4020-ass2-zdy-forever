A chart-interpretation session, applying this week's candlestick, volume and
trend vocabulary to real historical charts — with no simulated trade placed
against any of them.

## What you're given

A set of historical price charts (candlesticks and volume, unlabelled) drawn
from real market history, used here purely as reading material rather than a
trading decision.

## What you do

Annotate each chart: mark at least one trend, one range, and one
support/resistance zone, then write down what you'd still need to know
before treating any of those marks as a reason to trade — the lecture's
observation-versus-assumption line, applied by hand.

## What you produce

A set of annotated charts plus, per chart, one sentence on what's missing
between "I can see this pattern" and "this is a reason to act."

## Why it matters

Weeks 5 through 7 assume this vocabulary is already shared — nobody re-learns
what a support zone is once risk and trade plans enter the picture.
