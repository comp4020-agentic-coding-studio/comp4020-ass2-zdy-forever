A classification session: sorting a set of simulated trader profiles — and
your own circumstances — against the three styles from this week's lecture.

## What you're given

Short simulated profiles (available time per day, tolerance for watching a
screen, and stated goal) for a handful of hypothetical traders, alongside the
three styles: investing, swing trading, day trading.

## What you do

Classify each simulated profile against the style it actually fits, then run
the same classification on yourself — honestly, not aspirationally — and
name one risk that comes with the style you land on.

## What you produce

A completed classification table plus a short written self-assessment: your
style, your reasoning, and one risk you're accepting by choosing it.

## Why it matters

This self-assessment is background for the Market Mechanics Notebook, due
this week; it doesn't itself carry marks, but Weeks 6 and 7 assume you know
which style you're building toward.
