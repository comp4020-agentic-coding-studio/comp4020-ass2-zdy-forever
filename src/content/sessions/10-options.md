A payoff-reasoning session: drawing what a simulated call and put are worth
at expiry across a range of prices, then placing that shape next to Week 9's
straight line.

## What you're given

A simulated call and a simulated put, each with a strike price and a
premium, plus the straight-line futures payoff from Week 9 to compare
against.

## What you do

Draw the payoff diagram for each option by hand across a range of possible
expiry prices, marking the breakeven point and the flat region where the
premium is the only cost. Place both diagrams next to the Week 9 futures
line and state precisely where the shapes diverge and why.

## What you produce

Two hand-drawn payoff diagrams (call and put) with breakeven marked, plus a
short written comparison against the futures contract's linear payoff.

## Why it matters

This comparison is the working example the Strategy Lab and Rulebook
assessments can both draw on when discussing derivatives.
