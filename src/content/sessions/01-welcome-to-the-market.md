An orientation session: no market concept is tested yet, but everyone leaves
with the account every later session assumes exists.

## What you're given

A tour of how the twelve weeks fit together — stocks first, then styles and
risk, then leverage, futures, options and crypto — and access to the
simulated/paper-trading platform the course uses throughout. No real money
touches this course at any point.

## What you do

Set the simulated account up: confirm it loads, place no orders yet, and
work through the five asset classes the semester covers, naming one thing
each is actually used for rather than just its name.

## What you produce

A working simulated account, logged into and ready, plus a one-line note per
asset class in your own words.

## Why it matters

Week 2 assumes the account already exists and puts the first simulated order
through it — nothing here is graded, but nothing in Week 2 works without it.
