A critique session: unlike Week 6, you're not building a plan from scratch —
you're finding what's wrong with someone else's, using this week's spread,
slippage and VWAP material.

## What you're given

A simulated intraday trade plan, already written, that looks reasonable at a
glance but is missing or misusing at least one piece of day-trading-specific
context: no spread/slippage estimate, or VWAP treated as a standalone
buy/sell signal rather than one piece of evidence.

## What you do

Diagnose the plan: identify the bid/ask spread and a slippage estimate for
the instrument it's built on, check whether its use of VWAP and the day's
trend/range is doing the job of context or being treated as a signal on its
own, and state specifically what would need to change to make the plan sound under
day-trading conditions.

## What you produce

A written critique naming what's missing or wrong in the given plan, plus a
corrected version of the parts you flagged — entry, invalidation, sizing or
exit — with the spread/VWAP context now actually accounted for.

## Why it matters

The Strategy Lab asks for a day-trading plan built the right way the first
time; this session is where the failure modes get named before you have to
avoid them yourself.
