A trade-plan-construction session, combining the chart-reading vocabulary
from Week 4 with the risk toolkit from Week 5 into one complete swing plan.

## What you're given

Simulated multi-day price data for a shortlist of instruments, each with a
moving average already plotted, plus the annotated-chart skills from Week 4
and the position-sizing method from Week 5.

## What you do

Build a small simulated watchlist of at least three instruments, using the
moving average as one input among several — not a standalone signal — then
pick one and construct a full swing trade plan: entry, invalidation, position
size (calculated, not guessed), and exit.

## What you produce

A three-instrument watchlist with reasoning, and one complete swing trade
plan for the instrument you chose from it.

## Why it matters

This plan is a rehearsal for the Strategy Lab, not the submission itself —
Week 7 does the same construction at a faster, intraday pace before the
assessment asks for both.
