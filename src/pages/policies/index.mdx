# Policies and support

## Simulated markets, not real trading

Every price, order, chart and position used in this course is simulated.
Nothing taught here requires you to trade real money, and no assessment
depends on the outcome of a real trade — sessions and assessments use
supplied or simulated market material so that the mark reflects your
reasoning, not your luck.

## Not financial advice

This course teaches how retail markets and instruments work. Nothing in a
lecture, session, deck or assessment is personalised financial advice, and
nothing here should be read as a recommendation to buy, sell or hold any
real instrument.

## Handling market claims and sources

When a session or assessment asks you to use real historical data or refer
to an outside source, cite it plainly and don't present a simulated result
as if it were a real-world outcome. Course materials label simulated data
as simulated; keep that distinction in your own work too.

## Late work, extensions and academic integrity

Standard university policy on late submissions, extensions and academic
integrity applies to this course. If you need an extension, contact the
convenor before the deadline rather than after it.

## Getting help

Raise a question about a specific piece of work in the session it was set
in first. For anything else, see [People](/people/) for who to contact and
when.
